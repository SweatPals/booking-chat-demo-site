Find the class that fits you

Every Harbor class is coached, capped at twelve people and scaled to your level. Here is what to expect from each one.

Strength · 45 min

Harbor Strength

Squats, deadlifts, presses and carries with barbells and kettlebells. Programming runs in six-week blocks, so you can see your numbers go up. A short conditioning finisher closes every class.

Good for: building strength, people with some lifting experience · Runs: Mon 6:00am, Wed 12:10pm (Lunch Express, 30 min)
See times
Cardio · 45 min

Sunrise Row

Interval rowing on Concept2 ergs facing the water, mixed with short bodyweight blocks. Low-impact on the joints and easy to scale: every rower sets their own pace.

Good for: beginners, runners cross-training, early birds · Runs: Tue 7:00am, Sat 7:30am
See times
Recovery · 50 min

Tide Mobility

Slow, guided mobility for hips, spine and shoulders, finishing with ten minutes of breath work. Mats, blocks and straps are provided. No experience needed.

Good for: desk-bound backs, recovery days, all levels · Runs: Mon 6:15pm, Sun 9:00am
See times
Beginners · 60 min

Foundations

Our on-ramp. Learn the five basic lifts with light weights and lots of coaching, plus how to use the rowers safely. Take it once or for a few weeks before joining Harbor Strength.

Good for: first-timers, people coming back after a break or injury · Runs: Thu 5:45pm
