Good to know

Frequently asked questions

I've never lifted before. Is that OK?

Yes. Start with Foundations on Thursday evenings: it teaches the basic lifts with light weights, and our coaches scale every other class too.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, indoor trainers and a water bottle. We have lockers, showers and towels.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel up to 8 hours before class for a full credit back. Late cancellations and no-shows use the class from your pack or count against your monthly classes.

Is there parking?

Two-hour street parking on Commercial St, plus the Pier 4 lot ($2/hour). Bike racks are by the front door.

Do you offer private sessions?

Yes, one-on-one and small-group private coaching. Send us a message and a coach will get back to you within a day.
