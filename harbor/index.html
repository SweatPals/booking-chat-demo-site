Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is the morning Vinyasa really free?

Yes. Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7:00am are free community classes. Reserving a mat helps us plan.

I've never done reformer Pilates. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Saturday's class is capped at eight machines and the instructor sets everyone up. First-timers pay $15.

What should I bring?

Just yourself and water. Mats, towels and props are included; grip socks are required for Reformer.

Where do I park?

There's a free lot behind the building, plus street parking on weekends.

Can I pause my membership?

Anytime, for up to two months a year, from your account or at the front desk.
