Good to know

Questions we hear a lot

Is the morning Vinyasa really free?

Yes. Monday, Wednesday and Friday at 7:00 am are free community classes. Reserving a mat helps us plan.

What should I bring?

Just yourself and water. Mats, towels and props are included; grip socks are required for Reformer.

Where do I park?

There's a free lot behind the building at 1200 E 5th St, plus street parking on weekends.

Can I pause my membership?

Anytime, for up to two months a year, from your account or at the front desk.
